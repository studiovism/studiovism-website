About Studio Vism

Designing timeless interiors with purpose, detail and clarity.

Studio Vism is a Bangalore-based interior design studio creating thoughtful residential, commercial and retail spaces. Every project is approached with careful planning, refined detailing and a strong focus on how people experience a space.

We believe good design should feel effortless, remain timeless and improve the way people live, work and connect.

The Designer Behind Studio Vism

Ishika Shandilya Jindani

Founder & Principal Designer

Rather than following trends, Ishika believes every space should feel intentional, timeless and deeply connected to the people who experience it.

Every project begins with listening. Understanding how a client lives, works and dreams allows every design decision to have purpose rather than simply filling a space.

Whether designing a residence, healthcare environment or retail experience, her philosophy remains unchanged — creating interiors that continue to feel relevant for years, not seasons.
